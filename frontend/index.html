GuardedContext

A small guardrail + multi-provider routing layer for women's-health AI answers.

What this is: an independent research prototype built from public research (a published women's-health LLM benchmark and industry safety-consortium coverage — see sources in the accompanying write-up). What this is not: it is not affiliated with, connected to, or built on any real company's product or data, and nothing it outputs is medical advice.
